## Overview

Tomorrow's electricity prices are set in an auction every day, and they swing with demand, wind and sun. This project forecasts those day-ahead prices for Germany, France and the Netherlands, from raw market data to a deployed API.

Simple baselines set the bar first; machine learning models have to beat them in honest backtests before they are served.
